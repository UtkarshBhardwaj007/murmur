Murmur

Local, private dictation.

Microphone access is blocked

Murmur can't hear you: microphone access was denied. Enable Murmur in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone, then dictate again.

Enable auto-paste

Murmur needs the Accessibility permission to paste transcripts at your cursor. Click below, then enable Murmur in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility, and restart the app.

Shortcut

Press the shortcut, then speak. How it stops is up to you.

Speech model

Transcription runs entirely on this device. The model is downloaded once and everything works offline afterwards. Selecting a model that isn't downloaded yet starts the download and switches when it finishes.

Behavior

When off, the transcript is only copied to the clipboard.
